# ShortlistIQ Art Direction: Achromatic Ledger

**Chosen Direction:** `achromatic-ledger` (Light Theme)

### Design Rationale

ShortlistIQ is a high-stakes, data-dense B2B recruitment platform and consumer ATS evaluation tool where decisions directly affect real candidates' livelihoods. In accordance with anti-slop design principles and project constraints, we categorically reject dark mode, neon accents, glassy backdrops, brutalist heavy strokes, and playful decorative motifs. Instead, we select **Achromatic Ledger**: a calm, disciplined, and ultra-legible visual language rooted in archival balance, precise 1px hairline boundaries (`#E2E8F0`), flat surfaces without dramatic drop shadows, and high-contrast editorial typography powered by *Plus Jakarta Sans* paired with monospace numerals for tabular scores. Chrome and structural navigation remain quiet and monochromatic, ensuring 100% of visual salience is reserved for actionable candidate data, verbatim resume evidence, parse quality disclosures, and unambiguous semantic status indicators (verbatim icons + text for *Recommended Shortlist*, *Needs Review*, and *Recommended Not Shortlisted*). This establishes an environment of unshakeable institutional trust, technical honesty, and fatigue-free operational focus for recruiters and candidates alike.
